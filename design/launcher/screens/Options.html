Look
New renderer
On: the faster modern renderer, same look. Off: the Classic renderer, the original. Takes effect the next time you start the game.
Remastered ground
Richer shading and fine grain on the ground. Off brings back the flatter 2003 ground, which can be easier to read when you scroll fast.
Clearer water
You can see the sea floor through the water. Off draws the original solid water.
Moving water
Swell, beach foam, sun glints and brighter breaking waves. Off draws the original water surface.
RA3 water
Deeper water in the style of Red Alert 3: a teal sea and rivers, turquoise shallows along the shore, and ripples that catch the sun. Off draws the water the switches above set.
Smooth fog edge
The edge of the fog of war fades in about a second instead of popping.
Look governor
Helps hold your frame rate in big fights: when frames drop, it turns down particles, shadows and ground detail, then brings them back. Off keeps every effect at your settings.
No switch yet for grass and trees, or for the HD textures and 3D models: every install has them. These change only your own screen, so you still play with everyone.
